KITCHEN PHILOSOPHY

Wood from fallen orchards, heritage seeds, and patience.

We do not use gas or electric ranges for our core offerings. Every sauce reduction, sear, and bread roast occurs over glowing coals of seasoned hickory, white oak, and applewood.

•

Zero Single-Use Plastics

All our ingredients arrive in compostable wooden crates or glass containers from local regenerative farms.

•

Hand-Thrown Stoneware

Every plate and cup is crafted in small batches by ceramic artisans in rural Gujarat.

•

In-House Fermentation Lab

Koji garums, black garlic, and fruit vinegars aged 6 to 18 months for intense umami depth.
